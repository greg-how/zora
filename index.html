Zora

Zora is a private assistant that one household runs for its own use. It helps the household's adults keep track of their shared calendars, tasks, and notes.

Zora isn't offered to the public, and there's no way to sign up. Its Google sign-in is used only by the household's own Google account, to read the calendars the household chooses to share with it and to add or change events on the household's own calendars.
