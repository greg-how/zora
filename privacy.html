Zora privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Who uses Zora

Zora is a private assistant that one household runs for its own members. It has no other users, and it doesn't collect information from the public.

What Zora accesses from Google

Through the household's own Google account, Zora reads events on the calendars the household has chosen to share with that account, and adds or changes events on the household's own calendars when a household member asks. It doesn't request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

How the information is used

  • Only to answer household members' questions about their schedule and to keep their calendars up to date.
  • To answer a question, calendar details may be sent to an AI model service that the household operates in its own Microsoft Azure subscription, with conversation storage turned off. The information isn't used to train or improve AI models.
  • Zora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Calendar details may appear in Zora's conversation history and notes. These stay on the household's own computer, on an encrypted disk, and in encrypted backups that only the household can read. The information isn't sold, shared with anyone outside the household, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The household can remove Zora's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete Zora's stored data from its own computer.
